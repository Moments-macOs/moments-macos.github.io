Capture the Moment

Moments transforms your Mac into a professional digital PhotoBooth. Connect a Canon DSLR or use iPhone, iPad, or webcam to capture live photos. Display them instantly on monitors, AirPlay to Apple TV, or share remotely. Perfect for weddings, events, and studios—realtime photos become timeless memories.

Seamless Hardware Integration

Supports Canon DSLRs via USB, Nikon D5600, Canon 650D, and multi-camera setups. Add iPhone/iPad via Moments Camera app for wireless capture. Optional iSight webcam for live preview, dual monitors for dedicated display, and USB triggers with USB Overdrive for remote control.

Live Display and AirPlay

Photos appear nearly live on your second monitor or Apple TV via AirPlay. Guests see countdowns, PreRoll movies, and instant results. Fullscreen mode auto-advances images. Works on the same LAN—wired or wireless—for effortless big-screen sharing.

Effortless Workflow

Customizable PreRoll countdowns, flash, and PostRoll movies. Live Photos from iPhone save photo and video with matching filenames. Clean file naming avoids duplicates. Secure your setup in a custom enclosure with Mac, camera, and accessories for turnkey portability.

Plugin-Powered Reliability

Version 2.0.1 introduces a robust plugin system for cameras by manufacturer and device. Improved multi-camera support, manual refresh for Canon M50, and preserved original filenames from iOS devices. Built for stability in demanding event environments.

How It Works

1. Connect Your Gear

Attach Canon DSLR via USB to your Mac. Add iPhone/iPad wirelessly or use webcam. Set up USB trigger with USB Overdrive. Connect Apple TV or second monitor for display.

2. Configure Display

Position monitor or AirPlay to Apple TV on the same network. Dedicate the second screen to photos, enable fullscreen, and adjust auto-advance timing.

3. Capture and Share

Guests pose—trigger via USB button or app. See live preview, countdown, and instant photos. Images update realtime locally or remotely.

4. Review and Export

Playback sessions locally or save with original filenames. Precious moments preserved for later sharing.

FAQ

What cameras are supported?

Canon DSLRs via USB (tested extensively), Nikon D5600, Canon 650D in multi-setup. iPhone/iPad via Moments Camera app 1.5.1+. Webcams compatible.

Do I need extra software?

USB Overdrive for remote triggers. Moments Camera app for iOS wireless capture. AirPlay requires Apple TV on the same LAN.

Is setup complicated?

Minimal: secure Mac and camera in enclosure, connect displays. Preferences handle countdowns, timing, and display modes intuitively.

What about file management?

Photos save with clean, original filenames—no duplicates. Live Photos pair photo and video perfectly.
